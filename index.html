About

Designer. Project Manager.
Storyteller.

I came to interior design by way of architecture, construction management, and over a decade of hands-on project work across Vietnam and Australia. Before enrolling at DMACC, I managed complex commercial fit-outs for international hospitality brands — leading teams of architects, artisans, and contractors from concept to completion.

That real-world foundation shapes everything I do at DMACC. I don't just design spaces — I think about how they get built, what they cost, and how they perform for the people who use them.

My travels through Japan, Taiwan, Singapore, China, and Australia have given me a deep appreciation for how cultural context defines design quality. I'm drawn to the dialogue between Eastern materiality and Western spatial thinking — and I bring that cross-cultural perspective into every project.
